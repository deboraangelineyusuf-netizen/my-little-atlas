My Little Atlas

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use. Last updated: 19 November 2026.

My Little Atlas is a private, single-user personal journal for places, food, and wishlists. It is built and used by one person, its owner. It is not a public service, and there are no other users.

What the app does with Google user data

The app asks for one Google permission: drive.file. With it, the app can create and read only the files it creates itself in the owner’s own Google Drive (the photos the owner adds to the journal). It cannot see, list, or open any other file in the Drive.

What is stored, and where

  • Journal entries (places, food, wishlist, collections) are stored in the owner’s own Supabase database.
  • Photos are stored in the owner’s own Google Drive.
  • Access credentials are stored as private settings on the owner’s own hosting account and are never shown in the app.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. It is not used for advertising or to train any model. Nothing is public: the app requires the owner to sign in.

Retention and deletion

The owner can delete any entry or photo at any time. The owner can also revoke the app’s access to Google Drive at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Supabase project removes all journal data.

Terms of Use

This app is provided for the personal use of its owner, as is, without warranties of any kind. It is not offered to the public.
